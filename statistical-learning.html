Branch 01

Statistical learning for uncertainty and reliability.

I am interested in statistical tools that keep evidence honest when samples are limited, observations are noisy, and model outputs need uncertainty attached to them.

finite samples uncertainty diagnostics
Core question

When is a prediction or ranking reliable enough to act on?

This branch is the statistical base of the site. It connects inference, uncertainty quantification, effective sample size, numerical reliability, and the habit of asking what an estimate is allowed to claim.

Question

How should uncertainty be shown?

I want estimates to carry visible caveats: confidence intervals, sensitivity checks, diagnostics, and clear separation between signal and assumption.

Method lens

Reliability before polish.

The models matter, but so do data provenance, finite-sample behaviour, numerical stability, and whether the result survives a slightly different setup.

Evidence from applied projects.
