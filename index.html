Brief introduction / 简短介绍

A statistics background, moving toward decision systems.

I am a final-year BSc Statistics student at the University of Nottingham and an incoming MSE student in Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Johns Hopkins University. My current research interests move through three connected layers: statistics for uncertainty and reliability, bandits for sequential feedback and exploration, and LLMs as decision-making systems that need careful evaluation.
