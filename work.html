Research direction / 问题意识
01

When does offline evidence fail to rank bandit policies?

IPS and confidence intervals can be useful diagnostics, but I now treat this more as an exploratory question than a settled result.

IPS 和置信区间可以作为诊断工具，但我现在更愿意把这看作一个探索性问题，而不是定论。

02

Do LLMs explore, or do we just shrink their action space?

My current SUSTech preparation is circling this distinction: genuine exploration vs a pipeline that makes the choice easier by generating candidates first.

我现在的暑研准备正在区分：LLM 真的在探索，还是候选生成先把动作空间变小了？

03

How do we make experiments trustworthy when systems are adaptive?

This is where statistics, numerical reliability, and evaluation protocols meet: not only asking whether a model works, but whether the measurement is believable.

这里连接统计、数值可靠性与评价协议：不只是模型是否有效，而是测量本身是否可信。

Guided research-style project Jul 2025 - Sep 2025

IPS vs Regret in Offline Bandit Evaluation

I used this project to test a cautious question: how far inverse propensity scoring can go as an offline proxy for cumulative regret when evaluating bandit algorithms. The work compared Explore-Then-Commit, UCB, and Thompson Sampling on Yelp and MovieLens-style logs, using confidence intervals, ranking consistency, and effective sample size diagnostics. I would present it as useful training in offline evaluation rather than as a strong research claim.

这个项目更适合作为一次 exploratory 的训练：我尝试检验 IPS 在多大程度上可以作为 cumulative regret 的离线 proxy，用来评价 bandit 算法。项目比较了 Explore-Then-Commit、UCB 和 Thompson Sampling 在 Yelp 与 MovieLens 风格日志上的表现， 并使用置信区间、排名一致性和有效样本量做可靠性诊断。我不会把它包装成很强的研究结论。

Applied Math Modelling: Pendulum Rides and Thermostat Control

Two MGP modelling tasks sit behind this applied-math thread. In one, we modelled pendulum-type amusement rides, defining an excitement function from acceleration, jerk, and height under safety constraints. In another, we studied room heating with Newton cooling, heat-equation simulations, bang-bang and hysteresis control, and optimization of thermostat placement.

这一条 applied math 线索来自两个 MGP 建模任务：一个把 pirate ship / frisbee ride 建成摆类系统，用 acceleration、jerk 和 height 构造 excitement function，并在安全约束下 比较 motion design；另一个研究室内加热控制，从 Newton cooling 到一维 / 二维 heat equation、bang-bang / hysteresis control 和 thermostat 位置优化。
