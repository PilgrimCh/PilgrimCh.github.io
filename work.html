Research direction / 问题意识
01

Can offline evidence replace online regret?

IPS and confidence intervals are useful, but the hard question is when logged feedback stops being enough to rank sequential decision policies.

IPS 和置信区间有用，但真正的问题是：什么时候离线反馈不足以可靠排序序列决策策略？

02

Do LLMs explore, or do we just shrink their action space?

My current SUSTech preparation is circling this distinction: genuine exploration vs a pipeline that makes the choice easier by generating candidates first.

我现在的暑研准备正在区分：LLM 真的在探索，还是候选生成先把动作空间变小了？

03

How do we make experiments trustworthy when systems are adaptive?

This is where statistics, numerical reliability, and evaluation protocols meet: not only asking whether a model works, but whether the measurement is believable.

这里连接统计、数值可靠性与评价协议：不只是模型是否有效，而是测量本身是否可信。

Guided research project Jul 2025 - Sep 2025

Offline Evaluation of Sequential Decision Algorithms

I investigated whether inverse propensity scoring can serve as a reliable offline substitute for cumulative regret when evaluating multi-armed bandit algorithms. The project compared Explore-Then-Commit, UCB, and Thompson Sampling on Yelp and MovieLens datasets, using confidence intervals, ranking consistency, and effective sample size diagnostics.

我研究了 IPS 是否能在离线环境中替代 cumulative regret 来评价 multi-armed bandit 算法。项目比较了 Explore-Then-Commit、UCB 和 Thompson Sampling 在 Yelp 与 MovieLens 数据集上的表现，并使用置信区间、排名一致性和有效样本量作为可靠性诊断。

Bandits IPS Cumulative regret ESS Yelp / MovieLens

I built algorithms to compute the Daily Hydropeaking Index, processed national hydrological datasets from 2021 to 2023, and explored statistical and machine learning approaches for modelling hydropeaking behaviour.

Mathematical modelling Ongoing

2D Thermostat-Heater Control System

This project studies a two-dimensional heat diffusion model with Newton/Robin boundary conditions and localized heating. I use numerical simulation, bang-bang or hysteresis-style control, and optimization over thermostat placement to study temperature regulation.

这个项目研究二维热扩散模型、Newton/Robin 边界条件和局部加热控制。我使用数值模拟、 bang-bang 或 hysteresis 风格的控制策略，并通过优化 thermostat 位置来分析温度调节效果。
